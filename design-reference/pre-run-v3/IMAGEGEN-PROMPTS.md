# Run Quest warm-up artwork — v3 generation record

These are the final movement-specific prompts used for the v3 source sheets. The Yoga With Tim screenshots and supplied transcript are the pose authority; `recovered-mark/codex-clipboard-8fa49f87-0818-4d48-809e-320496fa1f0c.png` and `public/assets/yoga/mark-hero-v2.png` are the identity/style references.

## Shared art direction

Transparent-background instructional cutout of the same Yoga Mark: adult man with longer dark side-swept hair, black rectangular glasses, short beard, natural athletic proportions, charcoal tee, deep-sage joggers, black trainers. Full body visible, centered on a consistent 540 × 720 canvas, no text, no scenery, no duplicate limbs, no Tim likeness, clean natural joints, soft realistic illustrated finish.

## Ankle inversion / eversion

Show Mark standing tall with the working forefoot still grounded while the ankle rolls inward and outward. The lower leg stays stable; this is a small ankle inversion/eversion action, not a floating leg circle. Four readable phases, alternating sides only when needed for the animation.

## Hip flexion and opener

Show Mark balancing on one leg, lifting the opposite knee forward toward the chest, rotating the thigh outward, and catching the outside of the foot with both hands. Four clear phases. The final two frames are exact horizontal mirrors of the first two, including the head, hair, glasses, hands, legs, and shoes.

## Hip flexion with torso rotation

Show Mark standing with knuckles at the temples and thumbs pointed down, elbows open. Lift one knee and bring the opposite elbow toward that knee with a controlled torso rotation; arms stay in front of the head and never pass behind it. Include a neutral setup and opposite-side twist.

## Small arm circles — backward and forward

Show Mark standing with arms straight out at shoulder height and ribs stacked over the pelvis. First phase: palms facing upward while both arms make small, tight backward circles. Second phase: palms facing downward while both arms make small, tight forward circles. Keep the circles subtle; do not lift the arms overhead or make large windmill motions.

## Calf stretch and heel raises

Show Mark facing a wall with both hands supported, body long and feet stepped back. First phase clearly reads as a calf stretch with heels lowering; second phase shows the heels rising with control. Keep the wall support visible without scenery clutter. The cue retains the one-heel-at-a-time option.

## Forward fold

Show Yoga Mark in a relaxed forward fold, head hanging naturally, arms reaching toward the floor, legs only as straight as comfortable. Use the same longer-haired, glasses-and-beard Mark identity and charcoal/deep-sage outfit as the other v3 assets.
