# The Name Tags in the Cloud Archive

- Meditation: ego
- Duration: 540 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A cloud archive storing labels while awareness remains unfiled
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Rest comfortably and imagine a quiet archive floating above the city, storing every label the mind has collected.

## 01:00

Let your name appear on a small glowing tag. Notice that the name is known. What is aware of knowing it?

## 01:50

Add another label: successful... awkward... tired... kind... too much... not enough. Watch the archive receive it.

## 02:40

Ask gently: am I the label, or am I also the space in which the label is read? No final answer is needed.

## 03:30

Notice a thought uploading itself. Then notice the tiny gap before the next thought arrives.

## 04:20

Feel an emotion as data in the body: warmth, pressure, movement. What notices the signal before naming it?

## 05:10

[thoughtfully] The archive is useful. Memory and identity help you navigate. Yet the present awareness cannot be fully compressed into a folder.

## 06:00

Let all labels float at once without selecting one as the final truth. Rest in the capacity to notice them.

## 06:50

If confusion appears, notice confusion. If spaciousness appears, notice spaciousness. Neither receives administrator privileges.

## 07:40

Return to the ordinary body, with its history, needs, and name. Inquiry can make identity lighter without deleting it.

## 08:30

[softly] Let the archive drift into the clouds. Open your eyes and return to the useful world of names.
