# The Museum of Borrowed Costumes

- Meditation: ego
- Duration: 540 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Roles and identities displayed as useful costumes in a moonlit museum
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle and let breathing become ordinary. Imagine walking through a museum where every role you have worn is displayed as a costume.

## 01:00

Notice one role: friend, worker, child, helper, outsider, expert. See it hanging beneath a small museum light.

## 01:50

Quietly ask: what notices this role? Wait without replacing the question with a clever answer.

## 02:40

A memory appears like an old coat. It belongs to your history, yet it can also be observed from here.

## 03:30

A judgment appears: I am this kind of person. Place that sentence beside the costume and notice the space around both.

## 04:20

[thoughtfully] Roles are not false; they are useful clothing for particular rooms. The inquiry asks whether the wearer is larger than any single outfit.

## 05:10

Notice a sound, a sensation, and a thought. Each becomes an exhibit inside awareness. What is the open museum that contains them?

## 06:00

If the mind produces an answer, display that answer too. Let it stand beneath glass for a moment.

## 06:50

Drop the question and rest as simple noticing. Costumes remain, stories remain, and the room around them stays spacious.

## 07:40

[reassuringly] You do not need to lose identity. You can wear your name gently without sewing it to the sky.

## 08:30

[softly] Feel the body here. Leave the museum wearing whatever role the next moment needs, and open your eyes when ready.
