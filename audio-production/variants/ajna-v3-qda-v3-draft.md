# A Blue Window in the Snow

- Meditation: ajna
- Duration: 720 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A blue window glowing gently in a quiet snowy landscape
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit tall without becoming rigid. Let the face soften and place gentle attention between the eyebrows.

## 01:05

Imagine a small blue window glowing in a house surrounded by snow. It is simply a visual anchor, not a supernatural claim.

## 02:00

Notice the dark space behind the eyelids around that window. Let the image remain small and easy.

## 02:55

Breathe naturally. With each exhale, imagine snow settling without needing the world to become silent.

## 03:50

Thoughts may leave footprints across the field. See them, then return to the blue window.

## 04:45

If the forehead tightens, release the image and feel the whole face. Return only when the point feels comfortable.

## 05:40

Rest with the window for several breaths. No need to look through it or discover what is inside.

## 06:35

[thoughtfully] Attention can be both precise and gentle, like one lit square held by miles of soft weather.

## 07:30

Let wandering be another snowfall. Notice it accumulating, then gently clear a path back.

## 08:25

Release effort for a moment and rest in the whole inner landscape, window included.

## 09:20

Begin feeling the body below the face... throat... chest... belly... hands... legs.

## 10:15

Let the snow scene fade while sounds and temperature return to the foreground.

## 11:20

[softly] Open your eyes when ready. Carry the steadiness of one quiet window into the room.
