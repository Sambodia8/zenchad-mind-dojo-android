# The Last Train Enters the Depot

- Meditation: nsdr
- Duration: 780 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A night train settling carriage by carriage into a quiet depot
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Lie down and adjust anything that needs support. This is deliberate rest, not a substitute for sleep. Imagine the last train of the evening entering a quiet depot.

## 01:05

Feel the left foot and toes as the first carriage becomes still... sole... heel... ankle... calf... knee... thigh.

## 02:00

Let the whole left leg rest on its track. Nothing to lift, brace, or carry.

## 02:50

Move through the right toes... sole... heel... ankle... calf... knee... and thigh. The second line powers down.

## 03:45

Feel both legs together, heavy or light, detailed or vague. The depot accepts every kind of arrival.

## 04:40

Notice pelvis, hips, and lower back. Let the belly move without instructions while the central carriage settles.

## 05:35

Feel lower ribs, chest, shoulder blades, and upper back. With each exhale, another overhead light clicks off.

## 06:30

Travel through the left fingers, palm, wrist, forearm, elbow, upper arm, and shoulder. Let the arm be parked for the night.

## 07:25

Now right fingers, palm, wrist, forearm, elbow, upper arm, and shoulder. Allow both hands to release their imaginary luggage.

## 08:20

Notice throat, jaw, lips, tongue, cheeks, and the tiny muscles beside the nose. Let the face stop announcing where it is going.

## 09:15

Feel eyes, brow, forehead, temples, ears, and scalp. The destination board becomes blank.

## 10:15

Sense the whole head... whole torso... both arms... both legs. The train is complete and motionless enough.

## 11:15

[reassuringly] You may be awake, dreamy, restless, or nearly asleep. Rest does not require a particular platform.

## 12:25

[softly] Deepen the breath. Move fingers and toes, stretch if wanted, and return as the depot begins switching on its morning lights.
