# A Comet Does Not Need Chasing

- Meditation: urge-surfing
- Duration: 540 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: An urge passing through awareness like a bright comet
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Pause somewhere safe. This is for observing an ordinary urge, not replacing help in an immediate safety or medical situation.

## 01:00

Name what is here: craving... pulling... wanting. Imagine it as a comet entering the edge of your sky.

## 01:50

Locate the urge in the body. Notice pressure, heat, tightness, movement, or restless energy.

## 02:40

The comet can be bright without giving instructions. Watch its tail, speed, and changing shape.

## 03:30

Notice any thought that says now, always, or impossible. Let the words travel behind the comet rather than steering your body.

## 04:20

[reassuringly] Intensity may rise. A brighter moment does not mean you must chase it or make it disappear.

## 05:10

Rate the urge from zero to ten, then check again after several breaths. Observe change without demanding reduction.

## 06:00

Feel the body beneath the sky: feet, seat, hands, breath. You are more than the streak of light passing through.

## 06:50

Notice whether the urge has moved, sharpened, faded, or stayed. All are useful observations.

## 07:40

[thoughtfully] Choice can wait until the comet is no longer filling the whole window. Remain here for a few more breaths.

## 08:30

[softly] Let the comet continue beyond the horizon. Return to the room and choose the next action deliberately.
