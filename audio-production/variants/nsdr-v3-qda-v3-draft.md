# The Seaside Hotel Turns Down the Lights

- Meditation: nsdr
- Duration: 780 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A seaside hotel becoming quiet room by room
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Lie down comfortably and let the surface receive you. This practice offers deep rest while leaving sleep to do its own important work.

## 01:05

Imagine a seaside hotel after midnight. Begin at the left toes, foot, ankle, calf, knee, and thigh as rooms along the west wing go dark.

## 02:05

Feel the right toes, foot, ankle, calf, knee, and thigh. Curtains close softly along the east wing.

## 03:05

Notice both legs and the pelvis. Let their weight sink like luggage finally placed on the floor.

## 04:05

Move through lower belly, lower back, ribs, chest, and upper back. The lift stops humming; the lobby clock keeps time alone.

## 05:05

Feel left fingers, hand, wrist, forearm, elbow, upper arm, and shoulder. Let every key be returned to the desk.

## 06:05

Now right fingers, hand, wrist, forearm, elbow, upper arm, and shoulder. Both arms can be entirely off duty.

## 07:05

Notice throat and neck... jaw... lips... tongue... cheeks. Let the face become an unoccupied room.

## 08:05

Feel the eyelids, space around the eyes, brow, forehead, temples, ears, back of the head, and scalp.

## 09:05

Hear any distant sound as surf beyond the hotel windows. It can roll in and out without requiring attention.

## 10:05

Sense the whole body at once, from scalp to toes. The building rests beside the ocean and breathing continues like a quiet tide.

## 11:05

[thoughtfully] Nothing has to feel heavy. Nothing has to disappear. Let rest be the absence of another assignment.

## 11:55

Remain here for several breaths while the hotel keeps its patient watch beside the dark water.

## 12:25

[softly] Invite movement back into hands and feet. Open your eyes slowly, as though morning has reached the curtains.
