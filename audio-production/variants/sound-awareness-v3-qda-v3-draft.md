# Rain Builds an Orchestra

- Meditation: sound-awareness
- Duration: 405 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Ordinary sounds assembling and dissolving like a rain orchestra
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Let listening open. Imagine the room is an orchestra warming up beneath a roof of rain.

## 00:55

Find the lowest sound you can hear... a hum, engine, pipe, or distant vibration. Let it be the bass line.

## 01:35

Notice a higher sound... a bird, electronic tone, breath, or faint hiss. No need to hold it.

## 02:15

Listen for rhythm. Some sounds repeat; others arrive once and leave their chair empty.

## 02:55

Shift to the sound closest to the left... then closest to the right. Let attention move like a microphone on a slow rail.

## 03:35

Hear something in front... then behind... without turning the head or searching hard.

## 04:15

Notice silence as part of the arrangement. A rest in music is still held by the song.

## 05:00

Let every sound play together without choosing a favourite. The orchestra assembles itself and dissolves itself.

## 05:45

[reassuringly] If thought has been narrating, let that inner voice become one more instrument, then return to hearing.

## 06:15

[softly] Open your eyes and carry the wide listening field into the next minute.
