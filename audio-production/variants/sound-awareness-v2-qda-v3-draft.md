# A Forest of Small Transmissions

- Meditation: sound-awareness
- Duration: 405 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Forest sounds becoming a map of near and distant transmitters
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Close your eyes if comfortable and imagine every sound as a small transmitter hidden in a night forest.

## 00:55

Hear the whole forest at once. Let sound arrive before the mind decides what made it.

## 01:35

Choose the most distant signal. Rest attention there as though a tiny lamp is blinking beyond the trees.

## 02:15

Now find a middle-distance sound... something neither far away nor inside your body.

## 02:55

Tune to the nearest sound: breath, fabric, swallowing, or a quiet movement beside you.

## 03:35

Move from far... to middle... to near... allowing attention to travel without rushing.

## 04:15

Notice a sound beginning, changing, and ending. No transmission remains exactly itself.

## 05:00

[thoughtfully] Release the categories and hear one living field. The forest needs no conductor.

## 05:45

Include the body that is listening and the silence that appears between signals.

## 06:15

[softly] Open your eyes. Let the forest become an ordinary room again, still full of small transmissions.
