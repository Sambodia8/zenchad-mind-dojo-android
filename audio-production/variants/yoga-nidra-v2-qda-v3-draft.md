# The Starship Sleep Deck

- Meditation: yoga-nidra
- Duration: 1200 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A starship sleep deck powering down every body system in detailed sequence
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Lie down, support the body, and settle the temperature around you. Imagine entering the sleep deck of a starship travelling on automatic.

## 01:10

Feel the complete outline of the body. The ship is moving, but you have no controls to operate for a while.

## 02:05

Right thumb... index... middle... ring... little finger... palm... back of hand... wrist. Each panel turns from blue to dark.

## 03:00

Right forearm... elbow... upper arm... shoulder... armpit... ribs... waist... hip.

## 03:55

Right thigh... kneecap... back of knee... shin... calf... ankle... heel... sole... top of foot... each of the five toes.

## 05:00

Left thumb... index... middle... ring... little finger... palm... back of hand... wrist.

## 05:55

Left forearm... elbow... upper arm... shoulder... armpit... ribs... waist... hip.

## 06:50

Left thigh... kneecap... back of knee... shin... calf... ankle... heel... sole... top of foot... each of the five toes.

## 07:55

Both heels... calves... backs of knees... backs of thighs... pelvis... lower back... middle back... upper back... shoulder blades.

## 08:55

Lower belly... navel... upper belly... lower ribs... chest... collarbones... throat. The central deck grows quiet.

## 09:55

Jaw hinge... chin... lower lip... upper lip... corners of mouth... teeth... gums... tongue... root of the tongue. Let the mouth rest completely.

## 10:55

Cheeks... nose... nostrils... bridge... space around the eyes... eyelids... tiny muscles beneath the eyes.

## 11:55

Right eyebrow... left eyebrow... space between them... forehead... temples... ears... back of head... crown... whole scalp.

## 12:55

Feel the entire face powered down without disappearing. Breathing and swallowing can continue by themselves.

## 14:00

Whole right side... whole left side... both together... whole back... whole front... complete body inside the sleep deck.

## 15:00

[thoughtfully] A thought may cross the monitoring screen. Let automatic systems handle it without waking the crew.

## 16:00

Rest as one field of sensation suspended among distant stars.

## 17:00

Sense the breath entering and leaving the complete body, with no part needing to assist.

## 18:00

Remember the real room around you and the ordinary planet supporting everything.

## 19:20

[softly] Bring movement to fingers, toes, face, and limbs. Open your eyes when ready; the ship has carried you home.
