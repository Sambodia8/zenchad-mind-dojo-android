# The Train Can Leave Without You

- Meditation: urge-surfing
- Duration: 540 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: An urge arriving and departing like a neon train at a platform
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Find a safe pause. Imagine standing on a quiet platform while an ordinary urge approaches like a train in the distance.

## 01:00

Name the urge without boarding it. Wanting is here... impatience is here... a train is arriving.

## 01:50

Feel where arrival happens in the body: hands, mouth, chest, belly, legs. Notice the first vibration on the rails.

## 02:40

The train grows louder. Observe colour, pressure, temperature, and movement without stepping toward the door.

## 03:30

Thoughts may advertise the destination. Hear the announcement and remain on the platform.

## 04:20

[reassuringly] You do not need to fight the train. Let it be present at full size while breathing remains natural.

## 05:10

Notice the crest: perhaps urgency peaks, flickers, or changes location. Keep both feet connected to the ground.

## 06:00

Listen for the first sign of departure. It may be subtle: one loosened muscle, one quieter sentence, one second of space.

## 06:50

The train may linger or another may follow. Urges can repeat without becoming permanent orders.

## 07:40

[thoughtfully] Let your next choice come from the person on the platform, not from the loudspeaker.

## 08:30

[softly] Watch the red lights travel away. Feel the room around you and decide the next step in your own time.
