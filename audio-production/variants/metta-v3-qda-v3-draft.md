# Paper Boats Under the Moon

- Meditation: metta
- Duration: 570 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Paper boats carrying kindness down a moonlit canal
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Let the body be supported. Imagine a moonlit canal beside you, smooth enough to carry a small paper boat.

## 01:00

Write a wish for yourself on the first boat: may I be protected from unnecessary harm... may I know friendship... may I rest when rest is needed.

## 01:50

Set the boat on the water. If self-kindness feels difficult, let the canal hold the wish until you are ready to receive it.

## 02:40

Fold another boat for someone you love. Picture their ordinary face, including the details that make them human rather than perfect.

## 03:30

May you be protected from unnecessary harm... may you know friendship... may you rest when rest is needed. Watch their boat join yours.

## 04:20

Now choose a person who usually stays at the edge of your attention. A stranger has a whole weather system of memories too.

## 05:10

Offer the same wishes and let a third boat drift into the silver current. No emotion is required; intention is enough for this moment.

## 06:00

If it feels possible, include someone with whom life is complicated. Keep your boundaries. Simply wish that suffering and confusion do not keep multiplying.

## 06:50

[reassuringly] You may skip anyone who makes the practice unsafe. Loving kindness includes the person sitting here, and that person is allowed to choose distance.

## 07:40

Imagine the canal widening into a river filled with paper lights. May all of us find safety... connection... and enough gentleness for the next step.

## 08:30

Let the river travel beyond imagination. Feel your hands, your breathing, and the quiet place where the boats began.

## 09:00

[softly] Open your eyes when ready. Keep one blank boat nearby for a kindness you have not thought of yet.
