# The Moth Finds the Porch Light

- Meditation: focused-attention
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Attention returning like a moth to one warm porch light
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Choose one comfortable breath sensation as your porch light: nostrils, chest, or belly.

## 01:05

Feel one inhale and one exhale. Let attention rest near the small warm signal.

## 01:55

Imagine the mind as a moth circling through the dark. Wandering is what minds do.

## 02:45

When you notice thought, sound, or feeling has carried attention away, name it lightly and return to the porch light.

## 03:35

[reassuringly] The return is the practice. No flight path needs to be erased.

## 04:25

Stay with three breaths: one... two... three... feeling each from beginning to end.

## 05:15

Release counting and notice the most vivid detail of breathing.

## 06:05

If restlessness appears, feel it for one moment, then return without argument.

## 06:55

Again and again, the moth discovers the light. Each discovery is new.

## 07:45

Rest with fewer words now: breathing... wandering... noticing... home.

## 08:35

Widen attention to include the whole body and room.

## 09:25

[softly] Open your eyes. Let attention fly freely, remembering that it knows a way back.
