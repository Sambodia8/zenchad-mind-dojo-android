# A River for the Fallen Leaves

- Meditation: acceptance
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A slow river making room for every leaf without holding it
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Let the body rest and imagine sitting beside a slow river beneath autumn trees.

## 01:05

Notice the experience you are resisting. Name it gently if a name comes: fear... pressure... grief... wanting... no.

## 01:55

Feel where it lives in the body. Let sensation be a fallen leaf touching water for the first time.

## 02:45

Acceptance is the river making room for the leaf. It does not approve the storm that brought it down.

## 03:35

Notice the urge to push, solve, explain, or cling. Let that urge become another leaf beside the first.

## 04:25

Quietly repeat: this is what is here now... and this moment is allowed to be known.

## 05:15

Observe whether sensation moves, stays, widens, or changes temperature. No movement is required.

## 06:05

[reassuringly] The river is wider than one leaf. Include sounds, breath, contact points, and the room around the difficulty.

## 06:55

If a thought returns, let it touch the surface and travel at its own pace.

## 07:45

[thoughtfully] You can accept the reality of this moment and still choose boundaries, repair, or change in the next one.

## 08:35

Let the river continue without watching every bend. Feel the body beside it, breathing and supported.

## 09:30

[softly] Open your eyes when ready. Carry the river's spaciousness, not the leaves.
