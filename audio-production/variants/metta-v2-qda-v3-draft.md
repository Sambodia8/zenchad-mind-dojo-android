# The Firefly Post Office

- Meditation: metta
- Duration: 570 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A firefly post office carrying wishes through a summer night
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle into a position that feels kind enough for today. Imagine a tiny post office opening after sunset, staffed by patient fireflies.

## 01:00

Begin with a message addressed to you. May I be safe... may I be cared for... may I have moments of ease. Let the words glow without demanding that you believe them perfectly.

## 01:50

A firefly lifts the message gently and carries it around the quiet rooms of your body. Perhaps warmth arrives. Perhaps awkwardness does. Both can receive mail here.

## 02:40

Now picture someone easy to wish well. Place their name on another small envelope. May you be safe... may you be cared for... may you have moments of ease.

## 03:30

Watch that light travel across gardens, rooftops, and sleeping streets. You do not need to know how the message lands.

## 04:20

Bring to mind a familiar stranger... someone seen on a bus, in a shop, or through a neighbouring window. Their life contains rooms you have never entered.

## 05:10

May you be safe... may you be cared for... may you have moments of ease. Another firefly rises into the common dark.

## 06:00

[thoughtfully] Include someone difficult only if that feels workable. You can offer a modest wish without approving their actions: may wisdom and kindness become more possible.

## 06:50

Let the post office grow busy now. Thousands of small lights carry wishes toward animals, strangers, friends, and people awake beneath unfamiliar skies.

## 07:40

May we be safe... may we be cared for... may we have moments of ease. Let the sentence be a direction of the heart, not a promise about the world.

## 08:30

Release the words and feel the body breathing. The fireflies can continue their work without your supervision.

## 09:00

[softly] Return to the room. Open your eyes when ready, carrying one small undelivered kindness into the rest of your day.
