# The Campsite Check-In

- Meditation: grounding
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Checking sensory coordinates around a safe midnight campsite
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Keep your eyes open or softly lowered. Imagine checking the coordinates of a calm campsite after dark.

## 01:05

Find five things you can see. Colour, edge, shadow, texture, light. Let each become a marker around the tent.

## 01:55

One... two... three... four... five... You are here, in this particular place.

## 02:45

Notice four things you can feel: feet, clothing, seat, air, hands, or warmth.

## 03:35

Let contact points become tent pegs holding attention close to the ground.

## 04:25

Find three sounds. Perhaps one is distant, one is near, and one belongs to your body.

## 05:15

Notice two scents or two qualities of the air. Faint and neutral both count.

## 06:05

Notice one taste. Let the smallest ordinary sensation complete the campsite map.

## 06:55

[reassuringly] Thoughts can remain beyond the firelight. They are not the only coordinates available.

## 07:45

Rest with the strongest physical contact point for several breaths.

## 08:35

Look around and name where you are, the approximate time, and one gentle next action.

## 09:25

[softly] Pack only what you need. Leave the campsite slowly and return to the ordinary room.
