# A Small Kitchen at Dawn

- Meditation: grounding
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Five senses returning in a familiar kitchen at first light
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Let the eyes stay open. Imagine attention arriving in a small kitchen just before sunrise.

## 01:05

Notice five visible details as though morning light has found them for the first time.

## 01:55

Shape... colour... pattern... reflection... shadow. Let seeing return you to this room.

## 02:45

Find four touch sensations: fabric, floor, chair, temperature, weight, or the meeting of hands.

## 03:35

Notice three sounds, including the quiet mechanical sounds a home makes by itself.

## 04:25

Find two scents or air qualities. Perhaps clean, warm, cool, dry, familiar, or almost absent.

## 05:15

Notice one taste. Neutral is still a taste and ordinary is still present.

## 06:05

[thoughtfully] Let all five senses share one countertop of awareness. Nothing needs to dominate.

## 06:55

Feel both feet or the back of the body as one dependable surface.

## 07:45

Name the day, the place, and one thing you can do next without rushing.

## 08:35

Look around again and notice one detail that feels pleasant or simply okay.

## 09:25

[softly] Let morning continue. Move into the next moment with the room still around you.
