# A Candle Beyond the Spaceship Glass

- Meditation: trataka
- Duration: 740 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A candle appearing like a tiny sun beyond a spaceship window
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit with the flame near eye level. Keep blinking naturally and stop if the eyes become painful, dizzy, excessively watery, or persistently uncomfortable.

## 00:40

Imagine the flame as a tiny sun beyond the glass of a quiet spacecraft.

## 01:30

Rest the gaze near the wick. Let the surrounding room become deep space without forcing it away.

## 02:20

Notice flicker, colour, brightness, and the small dark centre. Gentle attention is enough.

## 03:05

Blink and return, as though the navigation window refreshes itself.

## 03:25

Close your eyes. Notice whether the tiny sun remains as an after-image behind the lids.

## 04:15

Watch it drift, pulse, reverse colour, or fade through the inner dark.

## 05:05

No image is also a complete experience. Rest with the window closed.

## 05:55

Feel breath and body while the eyes recover.

## 06:25

[softly] Open the eyes and find the flame again, one bright coordinate in a wide cabin.

## 07:15

Let attention stay near the centre without hardening the brow.

## 08:05

Notice that the flame changes constantly while remaining easy to recognise.

## 08:55

Rest with this small sun for several natural breaths.

## 09:25

Close the eyes once more and allow the outer flame to become inner memory.

## 10:20

Let the image travel beyond the window and disappear into a larger darkness.

## 11:15

Feel the whole face, whole body, and ordinary room returning.

## 11:50

[softly] Open your eyes slowly. Navigation is complete; take your time moving on.
