# The Museum of Unfinished Things

- Meditation: acceptance
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: An after-hours museum where unfinished experiences are allowed to remain
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle and imagine entering a museum after closing, where unfinished feelings and unanswered questions are allowed to remain on display.

## 01:05

Notice what is present now. Perhaps tension, sadness, restlessness, numbness, or something without a name.

## 01:55

Imagine placing the experience on a simple pedestal. Acceptance does not call it beautiful or permanent. It only stops hiding it in the basement.

## 02:45

Feel where the exhibit appears in the body. Notice shape, pressure, temperature, and movement.

## 03:35

The mind may arrive with tools and warning signs. Let the repair crew sit on a bench for a little while.

## 04:25

Quietly say: this is part of the room right now. It does not have to become the entire museum.

## 05:15

[reassuringly] If resistance appears, give it a small display card too. The wish not to feel this is also something being felt.

## 06:05

Broaden attention to sounds, breath, hands, feet, and the surface beneath you. More than one exhibit can exist at once.

## 06:55

For several breaths, allow the unfinished thing to remain without completing its story.

## 07:45

[thoughtfully] Acceptance is not permission for harm and not a contract with forever. It is honest admission to the room you are already in.

## 08:35

Imagine the gallery lights softening. The exhibit can stay while you walk toward the exit.

## 09:30

[softly] Move gently and open your eyes. Leave the museum without needing to take every unfinished thing home.
