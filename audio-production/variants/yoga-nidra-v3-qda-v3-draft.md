# The Cabin Under Quiet Snow

- Meditation: yoga-nidra
- Duration: 1200 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A warm cabin becoming still beneath deep snow through an intricate body scan
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Lie down and arrange the body for twenty minutes of unhurried rest. Imagine a warm cabin while snow gathers quietly outside.

## 01:10

Feel the whole body beneath its coverings. Nothing needs to be held up. The roof, walls, and earth are doing the work.

## 15:00

[reassuringly] You may feel heavy, light, restless, or nearly asleep. The snow does not require a particular kind of quiet.

## 16:00

## 17:00

## 18:00

## 19:20
