# Below the Blue Portholes

- Meditation: pratyahara
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: An underwater station gently closing its outer portholes
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Close your eyes if comfortable and imagine resting inside a quiet station beneath a blue ocean. Nothing outside needs to vanish.

## 01:00

Hear the furthest sound as though it travels through water to reach you. Receive the vibration, then let it continue past.

## 01:50

Notice nearer sounds... the room... clothing... breathing. Each is a small instrument panel reporting that the world is still there.

## 02:40

Imagine one outer porthole becoming softly frosted. Sight grows less important for a while, without being forced away.

## 03:30

Feel temperature on the skin, then let attention sink beneath it toward pressure, weight, and the slow movement of the belly.

## 04:20

Taste and scent may be faint. Let those channels rest too, like underwater microphones placed on standby.

## 05:10

[thoughtfully] The senses keep working; Pratyahara changes your relationship with their messages. You can hear a knock without opening every door.

## 06:00

Choose one inner sensation: heartbeat, breath, warmth, or the simple feeling of being contained by the body.

## 06:50

If an outer signal brightens, notice it and let it dim naturally. Return to the quiet pressure of the ocean around the station.

## 07:40

Rest in the inner room for several breaths. No perfect silence is necessary. Even the deep sea contains distant songs.

## 08:30

Begin clearing the portholes. Include sound, temperature, light behind the eyelids, and the shape of the whole body.

## 09:30

[softly] Move gently and open your eyes. Rise from the blue station slowly, keeping its quiet room somewhere within reach.
