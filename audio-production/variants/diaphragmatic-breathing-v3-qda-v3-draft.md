# The Cave That Breathes with the Tide

- Meditation: diaphragmatic-breathing
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A tidal cave expanding and settling with comfortable breath
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Find an easy position and let the breath arrive as it is. One hand may rest on the belly and one on the chest.

## 01:05

On an inhale, invite gentle movement beneath the lower hand. On the exhale, let the belly settle without pushing.

## 01:55

Imagine the lower ribs as a cave beside the sea. Water enters quietly and the walls make a little space.

## 02:45

The tide leaves in its own time. Keep every breath smaller than strain.

## 03:35

Notice expansion toward the front, sides, and back... a three-dimensional room rather than a balloon forced outward.

## 04:25

Let several breaths follow their own tide with no counting.

## 05:15

[reassuringly] If you feel dizzy, tingly, or uncomfortable, return to ordinary breathing immediately.

## 06:05

Try a soft count only if it helps: in one... two... three... out one... two... three.

## 06:55

Release the count and listen for the quiet point between waves, without holding the breath there.

## 07:45

Allow shoulders, throat, and jaw to remain unhelpfully relaxed while the diaphragm does less dramatic work.

## 08:35

Let the hands grow light and trust the body to continue breathing.

## 09:25

[softly] Leave the cave beside the tide. Feel the room and breathe however feels natural now.
