# The Ember in the Snow

- Meditation: trataka
- Duration: 740 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A warm ember resting within a silent field of snow
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Place the flame near eye level and sit comfortably. Blink whenever needed, and stop for pain, dizziness, excessive tearing, or lasting eye discomfort.

## 00:40

Let the flame become one warm ember resting in a wide field of snow. Keep the gaze gentle rather than rigid.

## 01:30

Notice the blue near the wick, the gold above it, and the soft halo around the edges.

## 02:20

Blink naturally. Each time the eyes reopen, meet the ember without trying to pin it in place.

## 03:05

Allow attention to rest on the quiet contrast between warmth and darkness.

## 03:25

Close your eyes. Notice any after-image floating behind the eyelids like an ember beneath snow.

## 04:15

Let the image change colour, move, fade, or disappear. There is nothing to preserve.

## 05:05

If no image appears, rest with the dark field itself and feel the body breathing.

## 05:55

Allow the eye muscles to soften completely.

## 06:25

[softly] Open the eyes and return to the flame. Notice movement without losing the centre.

## 07:15

The ember leans and rises, never holding one shape for long.

## 08:05

Stay with colour and light while allowing easy blinks.

## 08:55

Let the wide dark room support the small bright point.

## 09:25

Close your eyes again. Watch the remembered ember or the simple darkness where it had been.

## 10:20

Feel forehead, cheeks, jaw, and eyes becoming quiet as snow.

## 11:15

Widen to the whole body and the sounds around you.

## 11:50

[softly] Open your eyes slowly. Leave the ember in the flame and carry only the steadiness.
