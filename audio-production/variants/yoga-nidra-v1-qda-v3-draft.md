# The Greenhouse of Sleeping Flowers

- Meditation: yoga-nidra
- Duration: 1200 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A moonlit greenhouse closing flower by flower through a detailed body rotation
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Lie down and make every practical adjustment now. Let the body be warm and supported. Yoga Nidra invites deep rest; you may remain awake or drift near sleep.

## 01:10

Imagine lying inside a moonlit greenhouse where thousands of flowers are closing for the night. Feel the whole body resting as one quiet garden.

## 02:05

Bring attention to the right thumb... index finger... middle finger... ring finger... little finger... palm... back of the hand... wrist.

## 03:00

Right forearm... elbow... upper arm... shoulder... armpit... right side of the ribs... waist... hip.

## 03:55

Right thigh... knee... shin... calf... ankle... heel... sole... top of the foot... big toe... second... third... fourth... little toe.

## 05:00

Now left thumb... index finger... middle finger... ring finger... little finger... palm... back of the hand... wrist.

## 05:55

Left forearm... elbow... upper arm... shoulder... armpit... left ribs... waist... hip.

## 06:50

Left thigh... knee... shin... calf... ankle... heel... sole... top of the foot... big toe... second... third... fourth... little toe.

## 07:55

Feel both heels... backs of the legs... backs of the thighs... buttocks... lower back... middle back... upper back... both shoulder blades.

## 08:55

Notice the belly... navel... lower ribs... centre of the chest... collarbones... throat. Each flower folds inward without being forced.

## 09:55

Bring attention to the jaw... lower lip... upper lip... corners of the mouth... gums... teeth... the whole tongue resting wide and heavy.

## 10:55

Feel the inside of the cheeks... outer cheeks... nose... nostrils... bridge of the nose... space beneath the eyes.

## 11:55

Right eyelid... left eyelid... muscles around both eyes... right eyebrow... left eyebrow... space between the eyebrows.

## 12:55

Forehead... right temple... left temple... ears... back of the head... crown... entire scalp. Let every tiny facial muscle stop holding the day.

## 14:00

Feel the whole right side of the body... whole left side... back body... front body... the entire body at once.

## 15:00

[reassuringly] Nothing has to become numb or heavy. Sensation may remain bright. Rest is the permission to stop managing it.

## 16:00

Imagine moonlight moving slowly across the greenhouse roof while breathing continues beneath every closed flower.

## 17:00

Rest with the whole body as one field of quiet sensation, complete from scalp to toes.

## 18:00

Begin remembering the room around the greenhouse... its sounds... temperature... and the surface carrying you.

## 19:20

[softly] Deepen the breath, move fingers and toes, and stretch gently if wanted. Open your eyes as the greenhouse receives first light.
