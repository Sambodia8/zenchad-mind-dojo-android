# One Clear Frequency

- Meditation: focused-attention
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Tuning attention back to one clear frequency in a sky of static
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle and choose the breath as one clear frequency. Feel it where the signal is easiest to detect.

## 01:05

Listen with the body to one full inhale... one full exhale.

## 01:55

Thoughts may produce static, music, headlines, and weather reports. Notice the broadcast.

## 02:45

Turn the dial gently back to breathing. No need to smash the radio.

## 03:35

When sound distracts you, name hearing. When thought distracts you, name thinking. Then tune back.

## 04:25

[reassuringly] Every noticed distraction proves awareness is working.

## 05:15

Count five breaths if useful. One... two... three... four... five... then release the numbers.

## 06:05

Stay near the smallest breath detail you can feel without straining.

## 06:55

Let static rise and fall around the signal. You do not need perfect reception.

## 07:45

Rest in the repeated movement of losing and finding the frequency.

## 08:35

Expand hearing to every channel in the room while keeping a faint sense of breath.

## 09:25

[softly] Open your eyes and carry one clear frequency into the next task.
