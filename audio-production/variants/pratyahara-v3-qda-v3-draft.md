# The Library After Closing

- Meditation: pratyahara
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A vast library closing its sensory reading rooms for the night
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle as though you have entered a library just after closing. The building remains awake, but no new information is required.

## 01:00

Listen to distant sounds as books being returned somewhere far between the shelves. Let each thump and whisper belong to its own aisle.

## 01:50

Notice nearby sounds, then imagine the listening desk lowering its lamp. Hearing continues without becoming the main assignment.

## 02:40

Behind closed eyes, let the reading room of sight grow dim. Shapes and colours can remain like illustrations in books you do not need to open.

## 03:30

Notice touch at the skin... then deeper contact and weight. Choose the simplest sensation as a bookmark for attention.

## 04:20

Allow taste and scent to become background notes in the catalogue. Nothing is rejected; it is simply filed for later.

## 05:10

Turn inward toward breath, pulse, or warmth. Imagine a private reading room where the only text is the body happening now.

## 06:00

[reassuringly] A loud sound or thought may reopen a door. Gently close it after the message arrives, without blaming the librarian.

## 06:50

Rest between the shelves of inner sensation. There is no need to find a secret book or achieve blankness.

## 07:40

For a little while, let awareness read only one quiet page... inhale... exhale... pressure... warmth.

## 08:30

Now switch on the outer rooms again. Hear the building, feel the air, and notice light waiting beyond the eyelids.

## 09:30

[softly] Open your eyes and leave the library unhurriedly. The quiet page remains available whenever you return.
