# The Moonlit Accordion

- Meditation: diaphragmatic-breathing
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: The lower ribs opening like a quiet moonlit accordion
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit or lie comfortably. Place a hand on the belly if useful and let several breaths happen without changing them.

## 01:05

Notice movement beneath the hand. Invite the belly and lower ribs to make a little more room on the next inhale.

## 01:55

Imagine a moonlit accordion beneath the ribs opening easily... then folding home on the exhale.

## 02:45

Keep the breath quiet. The chest may move too; nothing needs to be locked.

## 03:35

Try an easy rhythm: inhale one... two... three... exhale one... two... three... only while comfortable.

## 04:25

[reassuringly] Release counting at the first sign of strain. Ordinary breathing is always available.

## 05:15

Feel the lower ribs widen to the sides and perhaps slightly into the back.

## 06:05

Let the accordion play almost silently for several breaths, with no need for maximum depth.

## 06:55

If dizziness, tingling, or discomfort appears, stop shaping the breath and rest.

## 07:45

Notice how exhaling can finish without squeezing. Let the folds close by themselves.

## 08:35

Remove the hand if you wish and see whether the body remembers the easy movement.

## 09:25

[softly] Release the exercise. Return to natural breathing and the sounds of the room.
