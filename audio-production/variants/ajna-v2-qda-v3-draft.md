# The Violet Compass

- Meditation: ajna
- Duration: 720 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A violet compass resting lightly behind the brow
- Approval basis: Expanded three-variant catalogue authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit comfortably and soften the muscles around the eyes. Let attention approach the space between the eyebrows without strain.

## 01:05

Imagine a small violet compass resting behind the brow. It does not predict the future; it simply gives attention a place to return.

## 02:00

Feel one inhale and exhale while the compass needle settles. Keep the gaze soft behind closed eyelids.

## 02:55

When thought pulls north, south, yesterday, or tomorrow, notice the journey and return to the centre point.

## 03:50

No colour or sensation is required. The compass can be imagined, faint, or absent while attention remains gentle.

## 04:45

Broaden briefly to the whole forehead, temples, and face. Release any pressure before returning to the small point.

## 05:40

[thoughtfully] Rest with centre and spaciousness together... one quiet needle floating inside a much wider sky.

## 06:35

If an image appears, let it change. If darkness remains, let darkness be complete enough.

## 07:30

Continue the simple cycle: wandering... noticing... returning to the violet coordinate.

## 08:25

[reassuringly] Clarity may come or not come. The practice is attention, not a promise of special perception.

## 09:20

Let the compass grow transparent. Include breath, chest, hands, and the contact points beneath you.

## 10:15

Hear sounds in the room while keeping a faint sense of the brow. Inner and outer coordinates can coexist.

## 11:20

[softly] Move your hands, open your eyes, and let the compass become an ordinary memory.
