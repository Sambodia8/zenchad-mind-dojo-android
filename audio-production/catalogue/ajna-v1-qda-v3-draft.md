# The Indigo Lighthouse

- Meditation: ajna
- Duration: 720 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A calm indigo lighthouse used as an optional point of attention
- Approval basis: audio-production/NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md approved 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit in a way that lets the spine feel tall without becoming rigid. Soften the jaw and the small muscles around the eyes.

## 01:00

Bring gentle attention to the space between the eyebrows. There is no need to cross the eyes, strain, or search for a special sensation.

## 01:50

If imagery helps, imagine an indigo lighthouse resting behind the forehead. Its beam turns slowly across a quiet inner sea, asking nothing from the horizon.

## 02:40

Feel one natural inhale... and one natural exhale. Let the point of attention remain light, as though a feather could land there without being held.

## 03:30

Thoughts may cross the beam like birds at night. There is no need to follow them or frighten them away. Notice the crossing, then return to the lighthouse.

## 04:20

[thoughtfully] The Ajna point is being used here as a focus for attention and imagination. You do not need to see colours, feel energy, or make anything happen.

## 05:10

Notice the darkness behind the eyelids. Perhaps it is flat, grainy, colourful, or changing. Let the indigo image remain optional within that wider space.

## 06:00

If pressure builds in the forehead, broaden attention to the whole face. Relax the eyes, feel the breath, then return only if the focus feels comfortable.

## 06:50

For a little while, rest with the small point and the wide darkness together... a lighthouse with plenty of ocean around it.

## 07:40

When attention wanders, let the return be unremarkable. No alarm, no self-criticism. The beam simply completes another quiet circle.

## 08:30

[reassuringly] You may notice clarity, sleepiness, restlessness, or nothing in particular. The practice is the gentle returning, not the production of an experience.

## 09:20

Begin widening attention. Include the forehead... the face... the throat... the chest... and the contact points beneath your body.

## 10:20

Let the imagined lighthouse grow distant while sounds in the room become clear again. Notice how inner focus and ordinary surroundings can share the same map.

## 11:20

[softly] Take an easy breath. Move your hands, soften your gaze, and open your eyes when ready. Let the small blue light remain only as a memory.
