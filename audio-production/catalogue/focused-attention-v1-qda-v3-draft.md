# A Small Satellite Comes Home

- Meditation: focused-attention
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Guiding one small satellite back to its orbit whenever it wanders
- Approval basis: audio-production/NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md approved 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Find a steady position and let the body be comfortable enough to fade into the background. Choose the feeling of breathing at the nostrils, chest, or belly as your anchor.

## 01:00

Notice one inhale from beginning to end... then one exhale. You do not need to improve either one. Your only task is to know when the breath is here.

## 01:50

Imagine attention as a small satellite entering an easy orbit around the breath. It does not need a perfect circle. It only needs somewhere to return.

## 02:40

Sooner or later, attention will wander. When you notice, quietly name what carried it: thinking... sound... feeling... planning. Then guide the satellite home.

## 03:30

[reassuringly] The moment of noticing is not a mistake. It is the exact moment the practice becomes alive. Return without scolding the mind for travelling.

## 04:20

Stay with the next three breaths. One... arriving and leaving... Two... arriving and leaving... Three... arriving and leaving.

## 05:10

Let go of counting. Feel the most vivid part of the breath, even if it is only a tiny coolness, movement, or pressure.

## 06:00

If restlessness appears, include it for one moment. Feel where it lives in the body, then return to the breath without beginning an argument.

## 06:50

[thoughtfully] Attention may leave a hundred times. A hundred gentle returns are not a broken meditation; they are a sky full of successful homecomings.

## 07:40

Rest with fewer words now. Breath... wandering... noticing... returning. Let the simple orbit continue.

## 08:30

Begin widening attention beyond the breath. Include the whole body, the sounds around you, and the space of the room.

## 09:25

[softly] Let the satellite roam. Move your hands and feet, open your eyes when ready, and carry the skill of returning into the next thing.
