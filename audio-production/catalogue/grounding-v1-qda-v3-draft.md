# Five Coordinates Home

- Meditation: grounding
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Returning through five sensory coordinates to a safe, ordinary room
- Approval basis: audio-production/NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md approved 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Let your eyes remain open or softly lowered. Feel the surface beneath you. This practice uses ordinary sensory details as coordinates back to the present room.

## 01:00

Begin by finding five things you can see. Let the eyes move slowly. Notice colour, shape, shadow, and small details without needing to name everything aloud.

## 01:50

One... Two... Three... Four... Five... Imagine each object lighting a tiny point on a map that says: you are here.

## 02:40

Now notice four things you can physically feel. Clothing on skin... feet on the floor... air on the face... the weight of your hands.

## 03:30

Let each contact be simple evidence of the present. Four quiet coordinates transmitted from the body to the little control room of attention.

## 04:20

Listen for three sounds. Perhaps one is distant, one is near, and one is so ordinary that the mind had edited it out.

## 05:10

Notice two scents, or simply two qualities of the air. If scent is faint, you might notice coolness and warmth instead. There is no need to force a result.

## 06:00

Notice one taste, even if it is neutral. Let this final coordinate be small and unremarkable. The ordinary world is allowed to be enough.

## 06:50

[reassuringly] Feel all five senses sharing the same room. Thoughts may still be loud, but they are no longer the only signal on the map.

## 07:40

Choose one steady contact point, perhaps both feet or the back of the body. Rest attention there for several easy breaths.

## 08:30

Look around once more and notice where you are, what time of day it seems to be, and one next action that can be taken gently.

## 09:25

[softly] Keep the coordinates with you: seeing, touching, hearing, air, and taste. Move when ready, one ordinary step at a time.
