# The Garden Beneath the Ribs

- Meditation: diaphragmatic-breathing
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A soft bellows and underground garden breathing at an easy pace
- Approval basis: audio-production/NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md approved 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit or lie in a comfortable position. If it helps, place one hand on the upper chest and one over the belly. Let the first few breaths remain completely ordinary.

## 01:00

Notice which hand moves more. There is no wrong answer. We are only meeting the breath where it is before inviting a little more space below the ribs.

## 01:50

On the next inhale, see whether the belly can gently rise beneath your hand. On the exhale, let it settle. Keep the breath quiet and comfortable.

## 02:40

Imagine a soft bellows beneath the ribs opening without force... then closing without collapse. The chest may move too. Nothing needs to be held still.

## 03:30

Try an easy count if it feels natural. Inhale for one... two... three... and exhale for one... two... three... There is no need to fill the lungs completely.

## 04:20

[reassuringly] If counting creates strain, release it immediately. Return to ordinary breathing. Comfort is more important than matching a rhythm.

## 05:10

Picture a small underground garden beneath the ribs. Each inhale lifts the leaves toward moonlight... each exhale lets them rest back into the soil.

## 06:00

Continue at your own pace. Belly rising... belly settling... a simple tide with no requirement to become deeper than it wishes.

## 06:50

Notice the lower ribs widening slightly to the sides and back. The breath can be three-dimensional, like a quiet paper lantern opening in every direction.

## 07:40

Allow several breaths without instructions. If dizziness, tingling, or discomfort appears, return to your normal breathing and simply rest.

## 08:30

[thoughtfully] Let the hands become light. Notice whether the breath can continue by itself, with the body remembering the path rather than being pushed along it.

## 09:25

[softly] Release the exercise. Feel the room around you, move gently, and let the garden breathe in whatever way feels natural now.
