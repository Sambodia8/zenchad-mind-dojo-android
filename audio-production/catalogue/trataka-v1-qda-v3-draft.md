# A Friendly Star in the Flame

- Meditation: trataka
- Duration: 740 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A tiny friendly star held in a flame, then remembered behind closed eyes
- Approval basis: audio-production/NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md approved 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit comfortably with the flame near eye level. Keep your face and jaw relaxed. Blink whenever you need to, and stop if you notice pain, dizziness, or persistent eye discomfort.

## 00:35

Let your gaze meet the centre of the flame without drilling into it. Imagine a friendly little star has come close enough to visit.

## 01:20

Notice colour near the wick... the brighter edge... and the faint glow around it. Keep the eyes soft. Blinking is part of seeing.

## 02:10

When attention wanders, return to one small detail in the flame. No need to freeze the rest of the world.

## 03:00

[reassuringly] Let this be steady rather than severe. A star does not ask the sky to strain around it.

## 03:25

Now close your eyes gently. Notice whether an after-image appears behind the eyelids. It may be bright, faint, colourful, moving, or absent.

## 04:10

If an image is present, observe it without trying to hold it still. Let it drift like a tiny satellite across the dark.

## 05:00

If there is no image, rest with the darkness itself. The practice does not require a visual effect.

## 05:50

Feel the breath and the contact points beneath you while the inner sky changes on its own.

## 06:25

[softly] Open your eyes and return to the flame. Let the gaze land lightly, as though recognising an old friend across a quiet room.

## 07:15

Notice movement inside apparent stillness. The flame leans, recovers, brightens, and narrows while remaining itself.

## 08:05

Rest attention at the wick for a while. Blink naturally. Each return can be gentle and immediate.

## 08:55

Let the flame be the only small task for these next few breaths... a warm point of light in a wide and patient night.

## 09:25

Close your eyes once more. Notice the remembered flame, the after-image, or simply the space where it had been.

## 10:20

[thoughtfully] Imagine the little star becoming quieter and more distant. You do not need to preserve it. Let fading be part of the meditation.

## 11:15

Broaden attention to the whole face, the whole body, and the sounds in the room. Allow the eyes to rest completely.

## 11:50

[softly] Rub the hands together if that feels pleasant, then open your eyes slowly. Leave the flame behind and carry the steadiness.
