# The Quiet Side of the Signal

- Meditation: pratyahara
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A quiet spacecraft dimming its outer instruments and turning inward
- Approval basis: audio-production/NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md approved 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Find a position that can remain easy for a while. Let your eyes close if that feels comfortable, and imagine you are seated inside a quiet craft travelling without hurry.

## 01:00

Begin with the furthest sound you can detect. Perhaps traffic, wind, pipes, or a room beyond this one. Let the outer instruments receive the signal without chasing its source.

## 01:50

Now notice sounds closer to you... the room itself... clothing shifting... the small machinery of breathing. Nothing needs to disappear. You are simply changing which dial receives your attention.

## 02:40

Notice light and darkness behind the eyelids. Colours may flicker like distant panels. Allow the visual channel to dim by itself, without squeezing the eyes or pushing anything away.

## 03:30

Notice temperature where air touches the skin. Then feel the quieter information beneath it: pressure, weight, and the steady contact of the surface supporting you.

## 04:20

[thoughtfully] Pratyahara is not a locked door. The senses may keep sending messages. Imagine placing those messages on a gentle night mode, while attention turns toward the cabin within.

## 05:10

Rest with the breath as sensation rather than sound. Feel a small movement at the nostrils, chest, or belly. Choose whichever signal asks the least effort from you.

## 06:00

If an outer sound becomes bright again, let it arrive. There is no failed withdrawal. Notice it... allow it... then return to the quietest inner coordinate you can find.

## 06:50

Imagine the craft crossing a region where the stars are hidden, yet navigation continues. For a few breaths, let simple inner sensation be enough to guide you.

## 07:40

[reassuringly] You do not have to become empty or perfectly still. Thoughts, sounds, and feelings can remain in the background while you rest nearer to the centre of experience.

## 08:30

Begin turning the outer instruments up again. Include the room, the air, the sounds, and the shape of your body as one complete field.

## 09:30

[softly] Move your hands and feet. Let the eyes open when ready, bringing a little of the quiet side of the signal with you.
