# The Sky Behind the Mirror

- Meditation: ego
- Duration: 540 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Reflections, windows, and the open sky that notices them
- Approval basis: audio-production/NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md approved 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle into an upright or supported position. Let breathing happen naturally. This inquiry is not asking you to erase yourself; it is an experiment in noticing.

## 01:00

Notice a thought as it appears. It may be a sentence, picture, memory, or plan. Imagine it briefly reflected in a dark window.

## 01:50

Quietly ask: who is aware of this thought? Do not rush to answer with another sentence. Let the question open like a window rather than close like a quiz.

## 02:40

A feeling may appear. Notice its texture in the body, then ask: what is noticing this feeling? Rest for a moment before the mind begins writing its biography.

## 03:30

[thoughtfully] Names, roles, and stories are useful reflections. Friend... worker... dreamer... person who got something wrong. See whether each description can be noticed without becoming the whole sky.

## 04:20

Notice that sounds are known... sensations are known... thoughts are known. What is the quality of the knowing itself? You do not need a mystical answer.

## 05:10

If the mind says, I found it, notice that thought too. Another reflection has appeared on the glass. Let it be present inside the wider field.

## 06:00

For several breaths, drop the question. Rest as simple noticing... open enough for a sound, a sensation, or a passing thought to arrive and leave.

## 06:50

[reassuringly] Nothing needs to dissolve. Your ordinary self can remain useful and intact. This practice only reminds us that a reflection is not the entire night.

## 07:40

Feel the body sitting or lying here. Include your name, your history, and the room around you as parts of experience rather than problems to solve.

## 08:30

[softly] Let the question go. Move gently, open your eyes when ready, and return as the very ordinary, wonderfully unfinished person you are.
