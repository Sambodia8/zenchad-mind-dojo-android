# Radio Constellations

- Meditation: sound-awareness
- Duration: 405 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Tuning an old celestial radio from far signals to near ones
- Approval basis: audio-production/NARRATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md approved 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle and let the eyes close if that makes listening easier. Imagine an old radio whose dial can travel across the entire night.

## 00:55

First, open hearing to everything at once. Sounds do not need names. Let them appear as bright points in a changing constellation.

## 01:35

Turn the dial toward the furthest sound you can detect. Rest there without leaning or searching too hard. Let distance reveal itself.

## 02:15

If another sound interrupts, include it briefly, then return to the far edge of hearing... the quiet transmission beyond the nearest walls.

## 02:55

Now tune toward the closest sound... breathing... fabric... a tiny movement in the room. Receive the near signal without making it louder.

## 03:35

Shift slowly between far and near. Far... near... Each return is a small turn of the dial, not a test of concentration.

## 04:15

[thoughtfully] Notice the spaces between sounds. Silence may not be empty; it can feel like the dark thread connecting each radio star.

## 05:00

Release the dial and hear the whole field together. Near and far signals share one wide sky, arriving and disappearing by themselves.

## 05:45

Include the feeling of your body listening. Sound, silence, and sensation can all belong to this single moment.

## 06:15

[softly] Let your eyes open when ready. Keep the radio wide for one more breath, then return to the room.
