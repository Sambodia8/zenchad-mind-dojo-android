# The Harbour of Listening Lights

- Meditation: sound-awareness
- Duration: 405 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Sounds appearing as navigation lights across a dark harbour
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Let hearing open like a dark harbour at night. You do not need to identify every vessel; simply notice where each light appears.

## 00:55

Find the furthest sound available. Let it blink at the horizon, faint but complete.

## 01:35

Move to a sound in the middle distance. Notice its texture, rhythm, and the silence around it.

## 02:15

Now hear something close: breath, fabric, a tiny mechanical hum. This is the lamp beside the dock.

## 02:55

Choose one sound and stay with it for a while. When attention sails away, guide it back by the same small beacon.

## 03:35

Let that sound go and choose another. Attention can turn gracefully without declaring the first sound wrong.

## 04:15

Notice sounds arriving from the left... then the right... ahead... and behind, if those directions are available.

## 05:00

Open to the entire harbour. Loud and quiet sounds share one field, appearing and disappearing by themselves.

## 05:45

[reassuringly] There is no perfect listening state. Even distraction is another brief signal noticed by awareness.

## 06:15

[softly] Feel the body in the room and let the harbour remain open as you return to seeing.
