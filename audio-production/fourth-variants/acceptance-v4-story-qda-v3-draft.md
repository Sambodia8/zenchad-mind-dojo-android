# The Repair Shop for Bent Constellations

- Meditation: acceptance
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A midnight repair shop where imperfect constellations are welcomed as they are
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Let yourself arrive without improving the moment first. Imagine a repair shop open at midnight for constellations that have become bent or incomplete.

## 01:05

Notice what is present in you now: tension, sadness, impatience, numbness, calm, or something without a name.

## 01:55

Place the experience gently on the workbench. Say inwardly: this is what is here right now.

## 02:45

Acceptance does not declare the damage beautiful or permanent. It simply turns on the lamp so you can see what you are actually holding.

## 03:35

Find the feeling in the body. Give it room around the edges, as though the workbench were wider than the object resting there.

## 04:25

Breathe beside it. You do not need to breathe into it, through it, or away from it. Company is enough.

## 05:15

Thoughts may arrive with urgent repair instructions. Thank the mind for trying, then return to the plain fact of this sensation.

## 06:05

[reassuringly] If the experience feels too large, look around the real room, feel your feet, and choose a smaller piece to meet.

## 06:55

Imagine the constellation still shining while one point is missing. Wholeness can include a gap without pretending the gap is not there.

## 07:45

Repeat softly: this belongs to the current sky... I do not have to like it... I can stop arguing with its presence for one breath.

## 08:35

Let the workbench, tools, and midnight windows fade. Feel the larger awareness holding every imperfect star.

## 09:30

[softly] Return to the room. Take the next kind and practical step, leaving the universe unfinished enough to keep becoming.
