# The Weather Balloon Dispatch

- Meditation: metta
- Duration: 570 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: Kind wishes carried between distant towns by glowing weather balloons
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle where you are and imagine a small weather station waking beneath a lavender evening. A silver balloon waits beside you, light enough to trust the sky.

## 01:00

Tie a message to its string for yourself: may I be safe enough to soften... may I befriend this life... may I find one pocket of ease today.

## 01:50

The balloon rises without asking whether you have earned the message. Watch it carry your words above chimneys and telephone wires.

## 02:40

Prepare another dispatch for someone whose happiness comes easily to mind. May you be protected... may you feel loved... may rest find your address.

## 03:30

Their balloon joins yours in the high blue quiet. Kindness can travel beside kindness without either one becoming smaller.

## 04:20

Now think of a person you barely know. They have private forecasts, sudden storms, and mornings when the sun surprises them too.

## 05:10

May you be safe enough to soften... may you know companionship... may there be ease among your unknown hours.

## 06:00

If it feels safe, include a difficult person at a great and comfortable distance. Wish only that confusion and harm lose some of their weather.

## 06:50

[reassuringly] You never need to invite anyone closer. Boundaries can be kind fences around a garden that is still learning how to grow.

## 07:40

Picture hundreds of luminous balloons crossing the night between hospitals, houses, oceans, and lonely windows. May all beings receive some kindness.

## 08:30

Let the dispatches travel beyond your view. Feel the ordinary breath and the body that launched them.

## 09:00

[softly] Return to the room. Perhaps carry one simple wish into the next conversation, like a folded map of better weather.
