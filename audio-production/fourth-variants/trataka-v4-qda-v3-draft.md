# The Lantern in the Crystal Cave

- Meditation: trataka
- Duration: 740 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A steady flame reflected through a calm crystal cave
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Place a real candle on a stable surface at a comfortable distance, away from anything flammable. Sit upright without becoming rigid.

## 00:40

If you have an eye condition, recent eye surgery, migraine sensitivity, or discomfort, skip the flame and use a gentle non-bright point instead.

## 01:30

Let the gaze rest near the brightest part of the flame. Imagine it standing inside a quiet crystal cave.

## 02:20

Keep the eyes soft. Reflections may shimmer, but the central lantern remains your simple place of return.

## 03:05

Blink whenever needed. This is attention training, not a contest with your eyelids.

## 03:25

Notice the flame's colour, shape, and tiny movements while the body stays broad and relaxed around seeing.

## 04:15

When thought carries you down another tunnel, acknowledge it and come back to the lantern.

## 05:05

Let the breath move naturally. The flame does not need your breathing to remain alive.

## 05:55

[softly] Close the eyes now and notice any after-image floating behind the lids.

## 06:25

Rest attention on the inner light while it changes colour, moves, fades, or disappears completely.

## 07:15

Open the eyes and return to the real flame. Let the outer lantern become clear again.

## 08:05

Stay for another gentle interval, blinking freely and relaxing the muscles around the eyes.

## 08:55

Notice that steadiness does not mean stillness. The flame dances while attention learns to remain.

## 09:25

[softly] Close the eyes once more. Receive the after-image without trying to pin it in place.

## 10:20

Release even the inner image. Rest in the dark cave of awareness for a few quiet breaths.

## 11:15

Open your eyes, look away from the flame, and let ordinary shapes and colours return.

## 11:50

[reassuringly] Extinguish the candle safely. Carry the practice as a calm return, never as strain.
