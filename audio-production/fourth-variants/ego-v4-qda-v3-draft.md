# The Theatre After the Applause

- Meditation: ego
- Duration: 540 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A quiet theatre where roles and costumes are set down after a performance
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle into breathing and imagine a theatre after the audience has gone home. The stage remains, but nobody needs to perform.

## 01:00

Notice a familiar role you play: helper, achiever, outsider, expert, troublemaker, or something else. See it as a costume resting on a chair.

## 01:50

The costume may be useful and meaningful. Still, ask quietly: when I am not wearing this role, what notices its absence?

## 02:40

A thought enters like an actor rehearsing one last line. Who hears the thought? Do not answer in words too quickly.

## 03:30

An emotion may cross the stage with dramatic lighting. What is aware of the lighting, the feeling, and the wish for it to change?

## 04:20

Notice the body breathing in the front row. Is awareness located in one exact seat, or is that another idea appearing?

## 05:10

[thoughtfully] Let the question remain open. This is curiosity, not an attempt to erase your history, personality, or practical self.

## 06:00

Imagine the stage lights dimming. Names, opinions, memories, and plans can rest backstage without being destroyed.

## 06:50

For a few breaths, experience simple noticing before the next line is spoken. Nothing needs to claim ownership of the silence.

## 07:40

Now allow your ordinary identity to return, like a favourite coat chosen consciously rather than mistaken for the whole sky.

## 08:30

[softly] Hear the room around you. The theatre is not empty; it is spacious enough for every role and the quiet behind them.
