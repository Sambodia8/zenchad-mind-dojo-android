# The Telescope Chooses One Star

- Meditation: focused-attention
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A small telescope repeatedly returning to one steady star
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Settle into a stable position and choose one place where breathing is easy to feel. This will be your single star.

## 01:05

Imagine a small telescope turning toward that sensation: nostrils, chest, belly, or another clear point.

## 01:55

Follow one inhale from beginning to end... then one exhale. Let the telescope remain light in your hands.

## 02:45

Attention will discover other constellations: plans, sounds, memories, and interesting mistakes. Notice where it went.

## 03:35

Name the distraction softly if useful... thinking... hearing... feeling... then turn back to the chosen star.

## 04:25

[reassuringly] The return is the exercise. Wandering does not cancel the meditation; it creates another repetition.

## 05:15

Stay with three natural breaths... one... two... three... and begin again whenever counting is lost.

## 06:05

Notice the exact instant an inhale appears and the instant it becomes an exhale.

## 06:55

Let effort soften. Clear focus can be interested and awake without becoming narrow or severe.

## 07:45

For a little longer, keep choosing the same star. Each return polishes the lens.

## 08:35

Now widen attention to include the whole breathing body while the original point remains somewhere within it.

## 09:25

[softly] Open your eyes when ready. Carry the skill of returning, not a demand to remain perfectly fixed.
