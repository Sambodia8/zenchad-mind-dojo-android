# The Night Market Map

- Meditation: grounding
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: The five senses mapping a friendly night market one coordinate at a time
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Keep the eyes open if that helps you feel present. Imagine the room as a quiet night market with many simple coordinates home.

## 01:05

Find five things you can see. Notice colour, edge, distance, shadow, or shine without needing them to be interesting.

## 01:55

Choose one visible object and study a detail you had not noticed before, like a tiny sign above a familiar stall.

## 02:45

Find four sensations of touch: feet on the floor, fabric on skin, air at the face, hands meeting an object.

## 03:35

Press gently into one reliable surface and feel it press back. Let that contact mark your location.

## 04:25

Listen for three sounds. Let each become a lantern placed somewhere on the map around you.

## 05:15

Notice two scents, or simply two qualities of the air if scent is faint: cool, warm, dry, fresh, familiar.

## 06:05

Notice one taste, perhaps neutral. You do not need to create a sensation that is not available.

## 06:55

[reassuringly] If any sense is inaccessible or uncomfortable, skip it and choose another visible or tactile detail. The map has many routes.

## 07:45

Say quietly: I am here, in this room, at this moment. Feel one breath arrive and leave while the market continues around you.

## 08:35

Choose the next small practical action: stand, drink water, open a window, send a message, or remain seated a little longer.

## 09:25

[softly] Let the imaginary lanterns fade. Keep the coordinates of sight, touch, sound, scent, and taste as you continue.
