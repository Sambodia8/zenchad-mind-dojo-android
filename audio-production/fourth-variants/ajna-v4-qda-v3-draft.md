# The Planetarium's Violet Lens

- Meditation: ajna
- Duration: 720 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A planetarium focusing a soft violet lens at the brow
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit or lie comfortably. Let the eyes rest behind closed lids and imagine entering a small planetarium after dark.

## 01:05

Feel the body supported first... feet, seat, hands, and the natural movement of breath. Grounding is the floor beneath every constellation.

## 02:00

Bring gentle attention to the space between the eyebrows. Keep the physical eyes soft and level, without trying to look upward.

## 02:55

Imagine a violet lens there, no larger than a coin. It does not burn or push; it simply gathers scattered light.

## 03:50

With each easy inhale, the lens becomes clear. With each exhale, unnecessary effort falls away like dust from a projector.

## 04:45

A thought may paint a comet across the dome. Notice it, then return to the quiet lens at the centre.

## 05:40

If you see darkness, colour, patterns, or nothing in particular, all are acceptable. The practice is attention, not special effects.

## 06:35

Rest with the point of focus while the rest of the face remains spacious... jaw loose, tongue resting, forehead smooth.

## 07:30

[reassuringly] If pressure or strain appears, widen attention to the whole head or open the eyes. Comfort is more important than intensity.

## 08:25

Let the violet lens float in a much larger field of awareness, like one friendly planet among uncountable dark miles.

## 09:20

Release the image. Keep only the calm precision of returning whenever the mind wandered.

## 10:15

Feel the hands and feet again. Notice the room as the planetarium slowly raises its ordinary lights.

## 11:20

[softly] Open your eyes when ready. Carry the lens as a metaphor for clear attention, not something you must keep seeing.
