# The Moonlit Aquarium

- Meditation: yoga-nidra
- Duration: 1200 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A detailed body rotation inside a tranquil moonlit aquarium
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Lie down and arrange blankets or supports until stillness feels possible. Imagine a moonlit aquarium after closing, blue and quiet, with no visitor left to impress.

## 01:10

Feel the whole body resting on the floor or bed. Make a simple intention to remain gently aware while every unnecessary muscle clocks out.

## 02:05

Bring attention to the right thumb... index finger... middle finger... ring finger... little finger... palm... back of the hand... wrist.

## 03:00

Move through the right forearm... elbow... upper arm... shoulder... armpit... right waist... hip... thigh... knee.

## 03:55

Feel the right shin... calf... ankle... heel... sole... top of the foot... big toe... second... third... fourth... little toe. Let the whole right side become heavy.

## 05:00

Now the left thumb... index finger... middle finger... ring finger... little finger... palm... back of the hand... wrist.

## 05:55

Move through the left forearm... elbow... upper arm... shoulder... armpit... left waist... hip... thigh... knee.

## 06:50

Feel the left shin... calf... ankle... heel... sole... top of the foot... big toe... second... third... fourth... little toe. The whole left side releases.

## 07:55

Notice both heels... backs of the legs... buttocks... lower back... middle back... upper back... shoulder blades, spreading like rays across the aquarium floor.

## 08:55

Bring awareness to the lower belly... navel... upper belly... lower ribs... chest... collarbones... and the soft hollow of the throat.

## 09:55

Feel the back of the neck... base of the skull... back of the head... crown. Let the scalp loosen beneath an imagined current of cool blue water.

## 10:55

Now soften the jaw hinges... lower jaw... chin... lower lip... upper lip... corners of the mouth. Let the teeth separate.

## 11:55

Release the tongue from its root to its tip... underside and upper surface... allow it to rest broad and quiet in the mouth.

## 12:55

Feel the right cheek... left cheek... right ear... left ear... nose... nostrils... and the faint movement of air passing through.

## 14:00

Soften the right eyelid... left eyelid... right eye... left eye... the tiny muscles behind both eyes. Let seeing switch off for now.

## 15:00

Release the space between the eyebrows... each eyebrow... temples... forehead... hairline... and the entire scalp once more.

## 16:00

Feel the whole face at once, smooth as glass after the last ripple. The throat, shoulders, arms, hands, chest, belly, pelvis, legs, and feet all rest together.

## 17:00

[reassuringly] Nothing needs to happen. If thoughts swim past like silver fish, let them cross the blue room without following.

## 18:00

Experience the whole body as one quiet shape, surrounded by space and supported from below. Rest here without searching for sleep.

## 19:20

[softly] Deepen the breath. Move fingers and toes, roll the head gently if comfortable, and open the eyes when the moonlit room feels near again.
