# The Moon Garden Folds Its Petals

- Meditation: pratyahara
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: A moon garden gently folding its sensory petals for the night
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Imagine sitting in a garden that blooms only beneath the moon. You do not need to silence the world; simply let the garden meet it gently.

## 01:00

Notice the furthest sound. A night flower receives it on one wide petal, then slowly folds the petal inward.

## 01:50

Notice nearer sounds... the room, your clothing, the breath. Another petal closes without rejecting anything it touched.

## 02:40

Behind closed eyes, colours and after-images may shimmer. Let them dim like greenhouse lamps switching off one row at a time.

## 03:30

Feel temperature along the skin. Imagine the garden drawing its delicate leaves closer, keeping warmth near the stem.

## 04:20

Taste and scent may be vivid, faint, or absent. Let those pathways curl inward like silver vines returning to a trellis.

## 05:10

Now include the whole outer world as weather beyond a glass roof. It can continue while you rest beneath it.

## 06:00

Turn toward an inner sensation: the pulse in a fingertip, movement in the belly, or the quiet weight of your hands.

## 06:50

[reassuringly] If a strong sensation asks for attention, respond kindly. Pratyahara is not disappearance; it is choosing where to place the lantern.

## 07:40

Rest at the centre of the folded flower. Sounds and sensations remain at the edges, softened by layers of patient petals.

## 08:30

For a few breaths, enjoy the inward garden without trying to make it perfectly still.

## 09:30

[softly] Let each petal open again. Hear the room, feel the air, and return carrying a little moonlit quiet inside.
