# The Jellyfish Bell

- Meditation: diaphragmatic-breathing
- Duration: 600 seconds
- Opening silence: 15 seconds
- Creative direction: The diaphragm moving like a luminous jellyfish bell in calm water
- Approval basis: Fourth variant for every narrated type authorized 2026-07-28

## 00:15

[softly] Sit or lie comfortably and let the first breaths be entirely ordinary. Imagine calm blue water beneath the lower ribs.

## 01:05

Place one hand on the upper chest and one around the belly or lower ribs, if touch feels helpful.

## 01:55

As air arrives, invite the lower hand to move a little. Picture a luminous jellyfish bell widening without hurry.

## 02:45

As air leaves, the bell gathers inward. Do not squeeze it empty; let the water complete the motion.

## 03:35

Feel expansion around the sides and back of the lower ribs as well as the front. The whole soft ring may participate.

## 04:25

[reassuringly] Keep each breath comfortable. If you feel light-headed, short of breath, or strained, return immediately to natural breathing.

## 05:15

Continue with an easy rhythm: widening... gathering... widening... gathering. No number needs to govern the sea.

## 06:05

Let the shoulders remain quiet while the deeper movement does less visible work below.

## 06:55

Notice whether the breath can become smoother rather than bigger. A small jellyfish still travels perfectly well.

## 07:45

Release the image and feel the actual body breathing, supported by muscles that already know the route.

## 08:35

Take several unshaped breaths. Observe what continues by itself when you stop helping.

## 09:25

[softly] Let the hands rest. Return to the room with the lower ribs spacious and the breath free to choose its own tide.
