## Script

### 00:00 — Arrive

[softly] Find a position that asks as little from you as possible.

Let your hands land somewhere easy... and allow the surface beneath you to carry the weight you have
been carrying.

For the next few minutes, there is nowhere else you need to be.

### 00:40 — Feel supported

Notice the places where your body meets the chair... the bed... or the floor.

Imagine those points of contact as quiet coordinates on a night-time map. You have arrived here. That
is enough.

### 01:15 — Let the breath happen

There is no need to manufacture a perfect breath.

Let the inhale find you... like a pale tide entering a sleeping harbour.

Let the exhale leave in its own time... while a row of distant windows quietly turns out its lights.

### 02:00 — Notice the inner weather

[thoughtfully] Somewhere behind your ribs, imagine a small observatory with its instruments switched
on.

It does not argue with the sky. It simply notices.

What is the weather inside you right now? Perhaps a drizzle of unease... an electric wind of
restlessness... or a clear patch you did not expect.

### 02:50 — Meet resistance

The mind may already be sending out a repair crew... trying to tighten a bolt, erase a cloud, or hurry
this moment into becoming another one.

See if the tools can rest on the floor for a little while.

Acceptance does not mean liking what is here. It means allowing this moment to exist before asking it
to change.

### 03:40 — Name what is here

Gently name whatever you notice.

Thinking...

Pressure...

Sadness...

Numbness...

Wanting...

Or simply... something.

Then add the words: this is what is here right now.

### 04:30 — Find its shape

Notice where the experience appears in your body.

Perhaps it is a bright pin on the map... a heavy moon behind the forehead... or a small engine humming
under the ribs.

You do not need the perfect description. Just become curious about its shape... its temperature... and
whether it stays still or moves.

### 05:15 — Four spacious breaths

[softly] Stay near that sensation while four breaths pass.

One...

Two...

Three...

Four...

There is nothing to win here. Each breath is simply another little square of time in which you did not
have to run away.

### 06:15 — Make a little room

See whether the feeling can have one more inch of space.

Not the whole universe. Not even the whole room. Just one more inch.

Let it stand beside you like a traveller who arrived wearing a rain-soaked coat. You do not have to
hand over the keys. You are only allowing it to wait beneath the same roof.

### 07:00 — Welcome the no

If part of you says, no... I cannot accept this... notice that part too.

The no is also weather.

It may rumble. It may flash. It may fill every tiny radio with static.

[reassuringly] It is allowed to be here without becoming the captain of everything.

### 07:45 — Let thoughts travel

Imagine each difficult thought as a small message sealed inside a glass envelope.

You do not have to open every one.

Watch them drift across a dark reservoir... catching silver light for a moment... then travelling
beyond the edge of your attention.

Another message may appear. Let that one travel too.

### 08:30 — Widen awareness

Allow the observatory doors to open.

Include the sounds in the room... the temperature of the air... the weight of your hands... and the
steady fact of the surface beneath you.

The difficult feeling can remain somewhere in this wider landscape. It no longer has to occupy every
star on the map.

### 09:15 — Acceptance for this moment

You are not signing a contract with forever.

You are practising acceptance for this breath... and now this one...

The inner weather may change, or it may not.

Either way, you have made a quiet place where it can exist without being chased through every corridor
of your mind.

### 09:45 — Return

[softly] Begin to move your fingers and toes.

When it feels right, let your eyes open.

Whatever weather comes next... remember that you are also the room it moves through.

Take your time.
